The Partners

Eighteen years of finding homes, quietly.

Kapoor & Iyer was founded in 2008 on a simple belief: that the most important purchase of a family's life deserves patience, discretion and honest advice. We work with a small number of clients at a time, and most of our homes change hands without ever reaching a public listing.

From heritage bungalows on Malabar Hill to weekend estates across the harbour in Alibaug, we know these neighbourhoods street by street, and the people who live in them.
